Not every wrong is a lawsuit, and not every lawsuit is one a trial lawyer should take on a contingency fee. When the honest answer is that I am not the right door, these are the doors I send people to. Every link goes to the organization itself, not to a middleman. I have no relationship with any of them; they are simply the places that help.

Free and low-cost legal help

Texas Access to Justice Foundation →
The largest funder of free civil legal aid in Texas; its site explains who qualifies and where the money goes.
Texas Access to Justice Commission →
Created by the Supreme Court of Texas to expand access to the civil courts for low-income Texans; self-help and pro bono programs.
TexasLawHelp.org →
Free legal information, forms, and a directory of legal aid programs by area, in English and Spanish.
State Bar of Texas Lawyer Referral and Information Service →
The State Bar’s referral line, for a lawyer in a field I do not practice, such as medical negligence, family law, or employment.
Houston Volunteer Lawyers →
Pro bono civil legal help in Harris County for people who qualify.
Lone Star Legal Aid →
Free civil legal services for low-income Texans across a large region including Houston.
Texas RioGrande Legal Aid →
Free civil legal services in South and West Texas, including the Rio Grande Valley and Eagle Pass.

Insurance and work

Texas Department of Insurance, consumer complaints →
Complaints against an insurance company, agent, or adjuster, including your own carrier’s handling of a claim.
Texas Division of Workers’ Compensation →
Workers’ compensation benefits, disputes, and finding out whether an employer is covered.
OSHA, file a safety complaint →
A confidential complaint about an unsafe workplace. Retaliation for filing is prohibited.
Texas Workforce Commission →
Unpaid wages, unemployment benefits, and workplace discrimination complaints.

Complaints about police and jails

Houston Police Department, Internal Affairs →
Complaints about Houston police officers. Complaints must be sworn and notarized; the page explains where to go.
City of Houston Office of Policing Reform and Accountability →
The city office that reviews policing complaints and policy outside the department.
Harris County Sheriff’s Office, complaint against an employee →
Complaints about Harris County Sheriff’s Office employees, including the county jail.
Texas Commission on Law Enforcement, complaint procedures →
The state agency that licenses peace officers; how it handles misconduct reports and what it refers back to the agency.

Records and victim support

TxDOT crash report purchase (CR-3) →
Buy your own crash report (the CR-3) online from TxDOT.
MADD Texas victim services →
Free emotional support, information, and help with the criminal process for victims of drunk-driving crashes; 24-hour helpline.
Texas Crime Victims’ Compensation →
State program that can pay medical bills, counseling, funeral costs, and lost wages for victims of violent crime, including intoxication manslaughter and assault.
