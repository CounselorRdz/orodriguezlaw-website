What Dispatch is

The Night Docket’s news strand: what happened on Texas roads and in Texas courts, reported from credible outlets and agency releases, with no names and no conclusions about open investigations, then connected to the entries that explain the science and the law. Dispatches stay on the front of the ledger for about ninety days and live here permanently after that, by month. The Spanish twin of every dispatch is in El Parte.
