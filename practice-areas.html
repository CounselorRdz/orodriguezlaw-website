Civil Rights

When the government crosses the line.

Police brutality, excessive force, wrongful arrest, false imprisonment, and civil rights violations under 42 U.S.C. § 1983. Government actors are not above the law. When law enforcement or government officials violate your constitutional rights, Orlando Rodriguez takes the fight to federal court.

  • Wrongful arrest & false imprisonment
  • Unlawful search & seizure
  • First Amendment retaliation
  • Jail & detention conditions

Car Accidents

Insurance companies have lawyers. So should you.

Serious car accident cases involving catastrophic injury, traumatic brain injury, spinal cord damage, and wrongful death. Insurance adjusters work for the insurance company — not for you. Orlando Rodriguez works for you, and only you.

  • Catastrophic & serious injury crashes
  • Traumatic brain injury (TBI)
  • Spinal cord & paralysis injuries
  • Wrongful death
  • Uninsured & underinsured motorist claims

Truck & 18-Wheeler Wrecks

Complex cases. Maximum accountability.

Commercial trucking accidents involve federal regulations, multiple liable parties, and corporate defendants with aggressive legal teams. Orlando Rodriguez knows the FMCSA rules, the black box data, and the tactics trucking companies use to minimize your claim. We don't let them.

  • 18-wheeler & semi-truck collisions
  • FMCSA violations & hours-of-service
  • Driver fatigue & impairment
  • Improper loading & cargo shifts
  • Trucking company negligence

Industrial Accidents

Refinery explosions, chemical plant accidents, oilfield injuries, construction site accidents, and industrial equipment failures. Houston is the energy capital of the world — and its workers pay the price when companies cut corners on safety. We hold them accountable.

  • Refinery & chemical plant explosions
  • Oilfield & pipeline accidents
  • Construction site injuries
  • Toxic exposure & chemical burns
  • Third-party industrial liability
