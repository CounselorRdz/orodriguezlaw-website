Practice Areas

We fight the cases that matter.

Serious injury and civil rights, in Texas state and federal courts. Every case handled personally: no hand-offs, no associates, no excuses.

Civil Rights

When the government crosses the line.

Police brutality, excessive force, wrongful arrest, false imprisonment: violations of the Constitution under 42 U.S.C. § 1983. A badge is not immunity. When officials break the rules they swore to enforce, the case belongs in federal court, and that is where I take it.

  • Excessive force & police brutality
  • Wrongful arrest & false imprisonment
  • Unlawful search & seizure
  • First Amendment retaliation
  • Jail & detention conditions

Car Accidents

Insurance companies have lawyers. So should you.

Catastrophic collisions, traumatic brain injuries, spinal damage, wrongful death. The adjuster works for the carrier. The friendly voice, the fast offer, the slow delay: all of it serves their bottom line. I work for you, and only you.

  • Catastrophic & serious injury crashes
  • Traumatic brain injury (TBI)
  • Spinal cord & paralysis injuries
  • Wrongful death
  • Uninsured & underinsured motorist claims

Truck & 18-Wheeler Wrecks

Complex cases. Maximum accountability.

Commercial trucking cases involve federal regulations, layered defendants, and corporate legal teams that arrive before the wreckage is towed. I know the FMCSA rules, the hours-of-service math, the black box data, and the tactics used to shrink a claim. Knowing the playbook is how you beat it.

  • 18-wheeler & semi-truck collisions
  • FMCSA violations & hours-of-service
  • Driver fatigue & impairment
  • Improper loading & cargo shifts
  • Trucking company negligence

Industrial Accidents

Houston's industrial corridor demands accountability.

Refinery explosions, plant fires, oilfield and pipeline accidents, construction injuries. Houston is the energy capital of the world, and its workers carry the risk when companies cut corners on safety. The cost of that shortcut belongs on their ledger, not your family's.

  • Refinery & chemical plant explosions
  • Oilfield & pipeline accidents
  • Construction site injuries
  • Toxic exposure & chemical burns
  • Third-party industrial liability

Serious Personal Injury

The cases other firms turn away.

Amputations, severe burns, permanent disability, wrongful death. Cases that alter the course of a life demand time, resources, and courtroom skill most firms will not spend. I spend them. That is the practice.

  • Catastrophic & permanent injuries
  • Amputations & severe burns
  • Premises liability
  • Product liability & defective equipment
  • Wrongful death
