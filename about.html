Orlando Rodriguez is a Houston trial lawyer. For 23 years he has handled civil rights violations, serious car and truck wrecks, industrial injuries, and catastrophic personal injury cases in Texas state courts and the Southern District of Texas.

Large firms sell a brand, then hand the file to an associate. He does the opposite. Every deposition, every hearing, every argument, personally. A limited caseload is the price of that promise, and he pays it on purpose.
