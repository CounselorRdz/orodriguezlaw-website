Severe Burn Injuries.

Severe burns are among the most painful injuries there are, with a long road of surgery, scarring, and rehabilitation.

Home / Practice Areas / Serious Personal Injury / Severe Burn Injuries

A serious burn does not end with the fire. It begins a long road of surgery, infection risk, scarring, and rehabilitation that can last for years.

Severe burns are widely regarded as among the most painful injuries a person can survive. Treatment can mean repeated surgeries and skin grafting, a constant fight against infection, disfigurement, loss of function, and the psychological weight that comes with all of it. The costs run long and the suffering runs deep, and a case has to account for both.

Burn cases often arise from the situations that also cause explosions, crashes, and industrial failures, so the work of finding who is responsible overlaps with those. What sets a burn case apart is the extraordinary length and cost of the recovery, which has to be proven with the same care as the fault, through the medicine and the future it foretells.

Common Questions
Why are burn injury cases treated as catastrophic?
Because severe burns bring an unusually long and costly recovery: repeated surgeries and grafting, infection risk, permanent scarring and loss of function, and lasting psychological harm. The case must account for that entire road.
Where do severe burn injuries come from?
Often from explosions, vehicle fires, and industrial failures. Finding who is responsible overlaps with those cases, while the burn injury itself demands proof of an especially long and expensive course of treatment.
